# Unslop

Edit the supplied text to remove distracting AI patterns. Preserve its facts, meaning, and intended voice. Read the [pattern catalog](references/patterns.md) for the concrete checks and stable rule numbers.

Prefer concrete verbs and ordinary words. Remove unsupported attributions, filler, forced symmetry, and generic conclusions. Split sentences when a reader must backtrack. Preserve precise domain terms, quotes, code, and deliberate stylistic choices when simplifying them would damage the meaning. Do not turn a writing pass into source research or unrelated skill edits unless requested.

Return the edited text, with a brief note about substantive uncertainty only when needed. Do not fabricate sources or measurements to make a sentence sound concrete.
